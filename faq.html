Frequently Asked Questions

Is this official ARK software?

No. This is an unofficial fan-made tool designed for ARK server administrators and players.

Does this work on all dinos?

It supports all creatures included in the tool’s database with defined colour regions, New dino's may not be added instantly.

Do I need a server to use this?

No. You can use it for design and previewing, but admin commands require a server or single player to use.

Why don’t some regions change?

Not all creatures support all colour regions. Disabled regions are not editable as they are not used in the game.

Can I share my designs?

Yes. You can copy or share the generated URL which preserves your configuration.
